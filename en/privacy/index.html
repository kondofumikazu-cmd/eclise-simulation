Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the iPhone, iPad, and Mac versions of Eclipse Simulator.

Effective date: August 29, 2026

Data collected by the developer

The app does not use advertising, tracking, usage analytics, crash analytics, or login accounts. It does not send or store personal data on a server operated by the developer.

Location

The app accesses location only after you choose Current Location and grant permission in the system prompt. It uses the resulting latitude and longitude to set the observing point. Permission is optional; you can use the primary features by selecting a point on the map or entering coordinates.

Favorite observing locations remain on your device and are not sent to the developer.

Apple Maps

The app uses Apple MapKit and Apple Maps for map display, place search, and reverse geocoding. Requests are processed by Apple services and are subject to Apple's privacy policy and applicable terms. The developer does not receive personally identifying analytics from Apple Maps.

On-device storage

Display settings, favorite observing locations, and calculation caches are stored in UserDefaults or Application Support on your device. The app does not provide account sync or back these values up to a developer server. If you delete the app, its app-container data is removed according to the operating system's normal behavior.

Export and sharing

You can export CSV, JSON, PNG, and PDF files. You choose the save location or sharing destination. After export, the privacy practices of that destination or sharing service apply.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal data and does not provide user-to-user communication or public user submissions.

This website

This site does not use its own analytics, advertising, cookies, or input forms. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process technical information required to operate the service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for details.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy responses will be updated and published on this page.

Contact

For a question about this policy, email kondofumikazu@icloud.com or use the support page. GitHub Issues are public, so do not include personal information there.

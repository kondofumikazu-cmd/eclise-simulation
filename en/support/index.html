Support

Support

Requires iOS/iPadOS 17 or later, or macOS 14 or later.

Contact

For a problem, usage question, or suggestion, open an issue in this public GitHub repository.

Create a support request

A GitHub account is required. Issues are public. Do not include your name, address, precise current location, Apple ID, passwords, API keys, or other personal or secret information.

Helpful details to include

  • Whether you use an iPhone, iPad, or Mac, and the model
  • OS version and app version
  • The solar or lunar eclipse event involved
  • Steps leading to the issue and any displayed error

Frequently asked questions

Is location permission required?

No. You can tap or click the map, or enter latitude and longitude. The app requests location permission only after you choose Current Location.

Why does a historical event use a modern map?

The background is present-day Apple Maps. It does not reconstruct historical or future borders, cities, roads, or coastlines.

Why can values differ from a NASA or other reference table?

Results vary with the ephemeris, lunar-limb radius, ΔT, Earth orientation, terrain, and atmosphere assumptions. Review the calculation conditions and sources shown in the app. The app does not guarantee definitive values for observation planning, navigation, or scientific conclusions.

Is the lunar-eclipse color a prediction?

No. The red color is a schematic aid for understanding eclipse progress; it does not determine the atmosphere or actual color of a historical or future eclipse.

Which export formats are available?

You can export selected-event data as CSV or JSON and the screen or window as PNG or PDF.
